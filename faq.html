Donating equipment

If you have gear to give.

Almost certainly, yes. Scuffs, faded colours and worn grips are all fixable. The things we cannot use are cracked helmets, snapped frames and anything with structural damage. If you are unsure, send us a photo and we will tell you honestly.

We collect from everywhere in Hyderabad. Wherever you are in the city, put your area into the donation form and we will arrange either a pickup or the nearest drop-off point.

Hyderabad is the only city we operate in for now. We would rather do one city properly than four badly. If you are elsewhere and want to help start Second Innings there, we would genuinely like to hear from you.

Usually not — we match equipment to whoever needs it most at the time. But if you have a specific school or academy in mind, tell us and we will try. For larger donations from a company or a club, we can usually arrange it.

Not at the moment. Second Innings is not yet a registered organisation, so we cannot issue tax-deductible receipts. We will update this page as soon as that changes.

We strip anything reusable — grips, screws, netting, laces, padding — and keep it for future repairs. The remainder goes to a recycler. Nothing is sent to landfill, which is part of why we describe what we do as extending the life of sport rather than simply giving things away.

Requesting equipment

If your children need it.

No. The equipment is given free and stays with you. We ask only that it is used by children and never resold.

Mostly, yes — but repaired and checked before it reaches you. Occasionally we receive new equipment, and that goes out too. Anything protective is inspected especially carefully; if it does not meet the standard, we do not pass it on.

It depends entirely on what has been donated recently. Some requests we can meet in a couple of weeks; others wait for the right items to come in. We will always tell you where you stand rather than leave you guessing.

Yes. Equipment wears out, and groups grow. Get in touch whenever you need to — an existing relationship makes it easier, not harder.
