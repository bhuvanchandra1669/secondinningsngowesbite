Give your old kit a second innings.

One bat. One pair of boots. A whole club store room. All of it is useful, and all of it takes about two minutes to hand over.

📍 We collect from everywhere in Hyderabad

Start the form What we accept

The list

What we can and cannot take.

Being specific here saves everybody a wasted trip. If something is not on either list, send us a photo and we will tell you honestly.

Yes please

  • Cricket bats, balls, stumps, pads, gloves, helmets (undamaged)
  • Footballs, boots, shin guards, goalkeeper gloves
  • Badminton rackets, shuttlecocks, nets
  • Basketballs, volleyballs
  • Running shoes in wearable condition
  • Kit bags, cones, bibs, whistles, stopwatches
  • Jerseys and sports clothing, clean and intact
  • Anything new and unused, obviously

Sorry, no

  • Cracked helmets or damaged protective gear — never safe to pass on
  • Bats with splits through the blade or a broken handle
  • Rackets with bent or snapped frames
  • Shoes with separated soles or worn-through grip
  • Anything mouldy, wet or heavily soiled
  • Gym and exercise machines — we have nowhere to put them
  • Trophies, medals and memorabilia
  • Equipment for adults only, with no youth size equivalent
A quick favour: please give things a wipe before handing them over. It is a small thing, but it makes a real difference to the volunteers sorting through several hundred items in an afternoon.

Three ways

Getting it to us.

Drop it off

The simplest option. Bring it to a collection point near you — we will confirm the nearest one when you fill in the form.

We pick it up

For larger donations, or if getting to a drop-off point is difficult, we will come to you — anywhere in the city.

Run a drive

Schools, colleges, housing societies, offices and clubs. You collect from your community, we come and take the whole lot in one go. How a drive works

Over to you

Tell us what you have.

You do not need to be precise. A rough idea is enough for us to work out the best way to collect it.

We usually confirm collections over WhatsApp.
