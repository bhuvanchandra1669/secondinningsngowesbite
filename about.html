A small idea, taken seriously.

Second Innings began with a simple observation: the equipment already exists. It is just in the wrong cupboards.

Our story

Why we started.

Write it as you would tell it to a friend. What did you see that made you start? Was there a particular child, a particular school, a particular cupboard full of gear that nobody was using? Where were you? Roughly two hundred words is plenty.

Then say why the name. “Second Innings” carries two meanings at once — a second life for equipment, and a second chance for a child who was never going to get picked without it. Say which one you meant, or say you meant both.

Mission

What we are here to do.

To make sport accessible to everyone by collecting old sports equipment, refurbishing it, and redistributing it to those who need it most.

The three things we believe

Talent is everywhere; equipment is not. Almost nobody misses out on sport because they lack ability. They miss out because a bat costs more than the family can justify.

Repair is better than replace. A restrung racket is as good as a new one to a child who has neither, and it costs a fraction as much — in money and in waste.

Local is not a compromise. The skills to fix this equipment already exist in every neighbourhood. Our job is to route work to them, not to replace them.

Where we work

All of Hyderabad, and only Hyderabad.

We collect from everywhere in Hyderabad. Wherever you are in the city, we can reach you.

One city is a deliberate choice, not a limitation. We would rather run Hyderabad properly — knowing the schools, knowing the repair shops, turning up when we said we would — than spread ourselves thin across four cities.

If you are somewhere else and want to help start Second Innings there, that is a conversation we would very much like to have.

Governance

The formal bit.

Legal status

Not yet registered. We are working through that process now, and this page will carry the registration type, number and certificate the moment it comes through.

80G certification

Not yet granted. Until it is, we cannot issue tax-deductible receipts, and we do not solicit financial donations through this website. We will say so here the moment that changes.

Safeguarding

We never publish a child’s name or photograph without written permission from their school and their family. Volunteers working directly with children are always accompanied.
