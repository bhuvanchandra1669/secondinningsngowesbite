If your children need equipment, ask us.

For government schools, academies, community coaches and NGO programmes. There is no fee, no catch, and nothing to pay back.

Apply for equipment Who can apply

Eligibility

Who we can help.

We are a small organisation, so we prioritise places where equipment will be used regularly by a lot of children rather than sitting in a cupboard.

You do not need to be a large or well-known institution. A coach running weekend sessions on a patch of ground counts just as much as a school with 400 students.

Government & low-fee schools

Applications come through the headmaster, principal or sports teacher.

Sports academies

Especially where you have identified a promising young player who cannot afford their own kit. Tell us about them.

Community coaches & NGOs

Anyone running regular, free or low-cost sport for children.

After you apply

What happens next.

  • Step one

    We read your application

    Usually within a week. If anything is unclear we will call you.

  • Step two

    A short conversation

    A quick call or visit to understand how many children will use the equipment, who will look after it, and what you need most urgently.

  • Step three

    We match what we have

    We are honest about this: we can only give what has been donated. Sometimes that means a partial match, or a wait until the right items come in.

  • Step four

    Handover in person

    We bring the equipment to you, note down what was given, and take a photo if — and only if — you are comfortable with it.

  • And afterwards

    We check back in

    A few months later we will ask how it is holding up. If something has broken, tell us — we would rather repair it than see it go unused.
