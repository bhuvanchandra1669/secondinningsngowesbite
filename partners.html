Who actually fixes this stuff.

Second Innings does not repair equipment ourselves — that skill already exists in neighbourhood sports stores. We just connect it to the equipment that needs it.

🏪 Not publicly named yet. We work with a small number of local stores already, but we list a store here only once they have agreed to be named publicly. That page is being built alongside those conversations.

Why route repairs through a store at all, rather than doing it ourselves? Because the money and the skill both stay in the same neighbourhood, and it is work these stores already know how to do properly.

For store owners

If you repair sports equipment for a living.

We would like to talk. Here is roughly what it looks like in practice.

We bring the volume

Batches of donated equipment, graded and ready to assess — not one-off repairs trickling in.

You set the pace

Repairs happen around your regular business, not on a fixed deadline we impose.

Local recognition

Once you are comfortable being named, this page and our Instagram credit your store by name.
