What we do with your details.

Short version: we use them to arrange the thing you asked us to arrange, and nothing else.

What we collect

Only what you type into one of our forms: your name, phone number, email address, city, and whatever you tell us about the equipment you are donating or requesting. If you are applying on behalf of a school, we also collect the name and address of that school and the number of children involved.

Why we collect it

To arrange a collection, process an equipment request, or reply to your message. That is the whole reason. We also keep a simple record of what was collected and where it went, because being able to say honestly how much we have moved is central to what we do.

What we do not do

We do not sell your details. We do not share them with other organisations. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

Photographs

We never publish a photograph or the name of a child without written permission from their school and their family. If you have given us permission and change your mind, tell us and we will take it down — no explanation needed.

How long we keep things

Contact details are kept for as long as we are in touch with you about a donation or a request, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case equipment needs following up. Records of what was collected and where it went are kept permanently, but without personal details attached.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email second.innings456@gmail.com and we will action it. There is no form to fill in.

Where the information is stored

When you submit a form on this website it is delivered by a service called FormSubmit straight to our team’s email inbox. We do not run a database — your details sit in that inbox and nowhere else, and only the Second Innings team can read them.

This website

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, an animation library from a public code network (jsDelivr), and delivers form submissions through FormSubmit. All three will see your IP address, as any website you visit does. We do not run analytics, and we do not set any cookies of our own.

Contact

Questions about any of this go to second.innings456@gmail.com.
