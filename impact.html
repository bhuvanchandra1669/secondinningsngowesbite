How we count

One item equals one countable piece of equipment. A pair of gloves is one item. A set of six stumps is one item. We count something only once it has actually reached a child — not when it is donated, and not when it is repaired.

What we do not claim

We do not convert equipment into a rupee “value delivered”, and we do not count the same item twice if it is repaired again later. Where a number is an estimate, we say so.

Transparency

Nothing to hide, and not much to hide it in.

Second Innings is a registered organisation. We do not yet hold 80G certification, which means we cannot issue tax-deductible receipts and we are not asking for money on this website.

What we ask for is equipment, time and introductions. When our registration status changes we will say so here, publish our accounts, and only then open a donations page.
