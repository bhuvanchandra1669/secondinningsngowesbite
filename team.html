Event Planning

Makes the collection drives actually happen. A drive at a school or an office can bring in more equipment in two weeks than a month of individual donations, and this is the person who runs them.

  • Collection drives at schools, offices and societies
  • Drive kits — posters, boxes, briefing material

How we work

Small team, clear lines.

Five roles means nothing falls between two people assuming the other had it. Every donation, every application and every handover has a name attached to it.

We are all volunteers, so most of the work happens on evenings and weekends. If you have written to us and not heard back within a few days, please do chase — it means something slipped, not that we are ignoring you.

The team will grow as the work does. Right now the most useful thing an extra pair of hands can do is help on collection days.

Room for a sixth.

We are small enough that one more committed person genuinely changes what we can take on.
