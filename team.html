Six people, six jobs, one city.

Second Innings is small on purpose. Everyone here has a clear remit, and between the six of us we can tell you where any piece of equipment went.

How we work

Small team, clear lines.

Six roles means nothing falls between two people assuming the other had it. Every donation, every application and every handover has a name attached to it.

We are all volunteers, so most of the work happens on evenings and weekends. If you have written to us and not heard back within a few days, please do chase — it means something slipped, not that we are ignoring you.

The team will grow as the work does. Right now the most useful thing an extra pair of hands can do is help on collection days.

Room for a seventh.

We are small enough that one more committed person genuinely changes what we can take on.

Volunteer with us Get in touch

Manvith

Executive Director

Sets the direction, holds the partnerships, and answers for everything Second Innings does. The person a school principal or a funder speaks to when they want to know whether we are serious.

  • Overall strategy and priorities
  • School, academy and store partnerships
  • Registration, compliance and governance
  • Representing Second Innings publicly

Sayaan

Operations

Runs the pipeline that turns a bat in a cupboard into a bat in a net. Collections, grading, getting things to the repair shops and back, and the handovers at the other end.

  • Pickups and drop-off points everywhere in Hyderabad
  • Grading equipment and quality checks
  • Coordinating refurbishment with partner stores
  • Storage, inventory and handover logistics

Bhuvan

Finance & Tech

Keeps the books straight and the systems running. Every number on our impact page traces back to a record this role maintains, which is exactly why we can stand behind them.

  • Accounts, budgets and expense records
  • Equipment records — what came in, what went where
  • This website and the tools behind it
  • Building the gear tracking system

Karthik

Social Media & Outreach

The reason anyone knows we exist. Finds the donors, tells the stories, and makes the first call to schools and academies who have never heard of us.

  • Instagram and everything we publish
  • Documenting collections and handovers
  • First contact with donors, schools and academies
  • Press, and getting people to forward our link

Aashini

Event Planning

Makes the collection drives actually happen. A drive at a school or an office can bring in more equipment in two weeks than a month of individual donations, and this is the person who runs them.

  • Collection drives at schools, offices and societies
  • Drive kits — posters, boxes, briefing material
  • Handover events and community camps
  • Volunteer scheduling on the day

Aadya

Creative Design

Makes sure everything we put out actually looks like us. From the logo on a collection box to the graphics on a handover-day post, if it has our name on it, it went through her first.

  • Visual identity — logo, colours and type
  • Design for posts, posters and drive kits
  • Photography direction on collection days
  • Keeping this website’s design consistent
