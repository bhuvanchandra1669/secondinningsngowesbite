Home / Corporate & CSR

A CSR project that is easy to photograph and easy to measure.

What we do sits neatly across two things companies are already reporting on: community sport and the circular economy. Every item we rescue is an item that did not become waste.

Why it fits

Community sport and the circular economy, in one project.

A collection drive or a sponsored batch produces something concrete: a number of items collected, a number refurbished, a number of children reached — with names and locations you can report on honestly.

We operate only in Hyderabad, which means every rupee and every hour your team gives stays local — the repairs happen at neighbourhood stores, and the equipment goes to schools and academies in the same city.

Concrete, not abstract

A pallet of old equipment leaving your office is a better photo than most CSR activities produce, and a truer one.

Reported honestly

We would rather show you a real, small number than an impressive one we cannot stand behind — see how we count.

Low overhead

We are an all-volunteer team. Nothing your company gives goes toward salaries or office costs, because we have neither.

Three ways in

Pick whatever fits your team.

Office collection drive

We provide the materials — boxes, posters, a briefing for whoever runs it internally. You provide the cupboard. Usually two weeks start to finish.

Sponsor a refurbishment batch

Cover the repair cost for a set number of items already in our pipeline, and we will report back on exactly where each one went.

Team volunteering day

A sorting session or a school handover, done together as a team. More useful than it sounds, and more memorable than a workshop.

Before you ask

What you get back, and what we cannot offer yet.

Please note: Second Innings is not yet a registered organisation, so we cannot issue a tax-deductible receipt for financial contributions. What we can offer today is a plain acknowledgement and a clear report on exactly what your contribution paid for and where it went — equipment, materials, logistics and volunteering time are all things we can put to work immediately.
